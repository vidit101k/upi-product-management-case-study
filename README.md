# Optimizing Digital Payment & Transaction Success in India's UPI Ecosystem

## Product Management Case Study

**Product concept:** UPI Transaction & Resolution Tracker  
**Primary feature:** Transparent Refund Tracking and Resolution  
**Category:** Fintech | Product Management | UPI

---

## 1. Project Overview

This is an industry-level Product Management case study focused on improving customer experience across failed, pending and delayed UPI payment journeys.

The proposed solution is a UPI Transaction & Resolution Tracker that helps customers understand what happened to their payment, track refund progress, view the latest confirmed status and access escalation when a refund is delayed.

The project explores how better transaction transparency and refund communication could reduce unnecessary customer-support contacts and improve customer confidence.

## 2. Problem Statement

UPI customers and merchants face uncertainty when payments are pending, failed, or debited without confirmation.

Lack of clear transaction-status communication, guidance and refund tracking can lead to repeated payment attempts, disputes and reduced trust.

We aim to improve payment transparency and resolution guidance across the UPI payment journey.

## 3. Proposed Solution

An integrated transaction and resolution experience centred around a Refund Tracking Dashboard.

The proposed experience includes:

- Transaction details and current payment status.
- Refund amount and refund status.
- Refund journey and latest confirmed update.
- Applicable expected resolution timeline.
- Delayed-refund guidance and escalation.
- Complaint tracking in a follow-up release.

## 4. Target Users

**Primary users**
- UPI customers experiencing failed or delayed payments.
- Customers whose accounts are debited without successful payment confirmation.

**Secondary users**
- Merchants requiring payment confirmation.
- Customer-support teams handling refund-related queries.

## 5. MVP Scope

### First release
1. Transaction details and payment status.
2. Refund information and journey tracking.
3. Delayed-refund handling and escalation.

### Follow-up release
- Refund-status notifications.
- More detailed complaint tracking.

Advanced AI-based refund prediction and payment-network development are outside the project scope.

## 6. Product Success Metrics

**Primary metric:** Refund-related support contact rate.

**Supporting metrics:**
- Average time spent checking refund status.
- Customer satisfaction with refund transparency.
- Status accuracy.
- Escalation success rate.

The actual pre-launch baseline must be measured before setting an improvement target.

## 7. Competitor Research

The initial research considered:

- PhonePe
- Google Pay
- Paytm

The research reviewed publicly documented transaction-status, refund-guidance, complaint-registration and escalation capabilities.

The opportunity is not to claim that existing applications lack these features. It is to investigate whether customers still experience uncertainty despite existing capabilities and whether a more integrated experience can improve the journey.

## 8. Product Strategy

The proposed launch approach is a controlled pilot with a small group of customers who have previously experienced failed or delayed payments.

The pilot will evaluate:

- Customer understanding of refund status.
- Accuracy and reliability of displayed information.
- Successful access to escalation.
- Changes in refund-related support contacts.

If the pilot demonstrates positive outcomes, availability can gradually expand to other eligible customers.

## 9. Key Product Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Incorrect refund status | Display verified information and timestamps. |
| Bank/payment-system integration failure | Show the last confirmed status and explain temporary unavailability. |
| Poor customer understanding | Use simple language and clear refund stages. |

## 10. Implementation Perspective

The industry-level proposal includes a separate implementation perspective for a bank such as ICICI Bank, potentially through its iMobile UPI experience.

This explores how an existing banking application could introduce refund tracking, status transparency and escalation.

It is a proposed implementation approach, not a claim about existing product capabilities.

This case study follows a structured Product Management process:

1. Problem discovery.
2. Competitor research.
3. Customer journey and root-cause analysis.
4. Product requirements and strategy.
5. Feature prioritization and launch planning.

The project is a proposed product solution, not a deployed application. Customer research and pilot testing are required to validate its assumptions and expected impact.
