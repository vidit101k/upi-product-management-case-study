# Competitor Analysis
## UPI Transaction & Resolution Tracker

**Competitors:** PhonePe, Google Pay and Paytm  
**Research method:** Public documentation review  
**Research date:** 15 November 2026

## 1. Objective

Understand how existing UPI applications help customers when payments fail, remain pending or are debited without confirmation.

The purpose is to identify existing capabilities, understand the customer-support journey and explore opportunities for improving refund transparency.

**Important:** This research is based on publicly available documentation. It is not a complete hands-on product audit.

## 2. Competitor Comparison

| Capability | PhonePe | Google Pay | Paytm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction history and status | Documented | Documented | Documented |
| Pending-payment guidance | Not fully verified in reviewed sources | Documented | Documented |
| Failed-payment/refund guidance | Some guidance documented | Documented | Documented |
| In-app complaint registration | Documented | Documented | Documented |
| Complaint-status tracking | Documented | Not fully verified in reviewed sources | Documented |
| Escalation mechanism | Documented | Documented | Documented |

### A. PhonePe

**Observed capabilities:**
- Customers can select a UPI transaction from History and raise a complaint.
- Customers can check complaint status through the PhonePe app.
- PhonePe documents a grievance-escalation process.
- Its grievance policy describes response and resolution expectations.

**Research observation:**

PhonePe already provides transaction-linked complaint registration and complaint-status visibility. Therefore, our proposal cannot claim that these capabilities are missing.

**Sources:**
- [PhonePe UPI Terms and Conditions](https://www.phonepe.com/terms-conditions/upi/)
- [PhonePe Grievance Policy](https://www.phonepe.com/grievance-policy/)

### B. Google Pay

**Observed capabilities:**
- Customers can view transaction history and transaction status.
- For processing payments, Google Pay advises customers not to retry the same payment immediately.
- Its help documentation provides failed-payment refund guidance.
- Customers can raise transaction disputes through the app.
- Google Pay provides a grievance-escalation process.

**Research observation:**

Google Pay already addresses several important customer concerns, including payment-status visibility, pending-payment guidance and dispute registration.

**Sources:**
- [Google Pay: Check transaction status](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/16920039)
- [Google Pay: Raise a dispute](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/9777953)
- [Google Pay India: Grievances](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/10084701)

### C. Paytm

**Observed capabilities:**
- Customers can view transaction status through transaction history or Passbook.
- Paytm provides guidance for failed and pending UPI transactions.
- Customers can raise complaints for failed payments and money debited but not credited.
- Customers can track complaint status and view updates through Help & Support.
- Paytm documents escalation procedures.

**Research observation:**

Paytm already documents several capabilities closely related to our proposed product, including refund guidance and complaint tracking.

**Sources:**
- [Paytm: Failed and pending UPI transactions](https://paytm.com/support/upi/steps-for-resolving-failed-or-pending-upi-transactions)
- [Paytm: Raise a UPI complaint](https://paytm.com/support/upi/how-to-raise-upi-complaint-paytm)
- [Paytm: Track UPI complaint status](https://paytm.com/support/upi/track-upi-complaint-status-paytm)

## 3. Key Competitor Findings

Three important findings emerged:

1. **Transaction visibility already exists:** Competitors provide transaction history and status information.
2. **Complaint mechanisms already exist:** Customers can raise disputes and access support through existing applications.
3. **Refund guidance already exists:** Competitors publish guidance on failed payments, refund processing and escalation.

Therefore, simply building another transaction-history screen or complaint-registration feature would not be a sufficiently differentiated product proposal.

## 4. Identified Product Opportunity

Our opportunity is to investigate whether customers still experience uncertainty despite existing capabilities.

The proposed product experience brings together:

- Original transaction information.
- Current confirmed payment status.
- Refund amount and refund journey.
- Last confirmed update.
- Applicable expected resolution timeline.
- Clear guidance when the refund is delayed.
- Complaint escalation and tracking.

The proposed differentiation is an integrated and understandable refund-resolution experience, rather than the invention of individual features that competitors may already offer.

## 5. Customer Research Required

Public documentation cannot establish how effectively customers use these features or whether they remain confused.

We should validate the following questions through customer interviews or surveys:

1. Can customers understand the difference between pending, failed and refunded transactions?
2. Do customers know when to wait and when to contact support?
3. Can customers easily find their refund status?
4. Do customers understand the expected refund timeline?
5. Can customers track complaints without repeatedly contacting support?

These questions will help determine whether the proposed experience solves a real customer problem.

## 6. Product Strategy Implication

The competitor research supports further investigation of an integrated Refund Tracking Dashboard.

Our strategy should focus on:

- Reducing uncertainty.
- Improving refund-status clarity.
- Providing reliable progress information.
- Making delayed-refund escalation easier.
- Reducing unnecessary refund-related support contacts.

**Conclusion:** The opportunity is not that existing UPI applications have no refund or complaint features. The opportunity is to investigate whether a more connected, reliable and understandable refund-resolution experience can improve customer confidence and reduce unnecessary support interactions.
